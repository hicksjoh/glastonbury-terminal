## Verdict

Not production ready as a decision tool. Infrastructure is solid: nothing crashes, auth gates hold, live-order guards hold. The problem is the same class as last time, one layer deeper: screens that render confident numbers from defaults, synthetic data, or nothing, plus three automations that have been silently dead.
